Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

1. Business Problem

Hospital Emergency Rooms handle a large volume of patients, making it difficult for management to monitor operational performance from raw patient-level data.

The objective of this project was to analyze patient volume, waiting time, patient satisfaction, admission patterns, department referrals, and patient traffic and evaluate whether patients were being seen within the 30-minute target.

2. Data Cleaning & Transformation

- Imported the Hospital Emergency Room dataset into Power BI.
- Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
- Prepared patient, admission, waiting-time, satisfaction, demographic, and referral fields for analysis.
- Structured the data model for accurate calculations and interactive reporting.
- Created DAX measures and KPIs to analyze patient volume, waiting time, satisfaction, referrals, and admission patterns.
  
3. Steps of Analysis
   
- Analyzed total patient volume and average waiting time.
- Evaluated the percentage of patients seen within the 30-minute target.
- Compared admitted vs. non-admitted patients.
- Analyzed patient distribution by age group, gender, and race.
- Identified departments receiving the highest number of ER referrals.
- Analyzed patient traffic by day and hour to identify busy periods.
- Tracked patient satisfaction alongside operational metrics.
- Built Monthly View, Consolidated View, and Patient Details pages for different levels of analysis.
  
4. Data Insights

- Average patient waiting time was approximately 35 minutes, exceeding the 30-minute target by around 5 minutes.
- Approximately 57% of patients were seen within 30 minutes, while around 43% exceeded the target.
- Patient traffic varied by day and hour, highlighting opportunities for improved staffing and resource allocation.
- General Practice and Orthopedics were among the major referral destinations.
- Patient volume was distributed across multiple age groups, providing insight into the ER patient population.

5. Recommendations

- Focus on reducing the average waiting time from approximately 35 minutes toward the 30-minute target.
- Analyze peak patient traffic periods and align staffing and resources accordingly.
- Investigate the causes of delays affecting the 43% of patients exceeding the 30-minute target.
- Monitor high-volume referral departments to improve patient flow and resource planning.
- Track patient satisfaction alongside waiting time to identify opportunities to improve the patient experience.

6. Tools & Skills Used

Tools: Power BI, Power Query

Skills: Data Cleaning, Data Transformation, Data Modeling, DAX, KPI Analysis, Data Analysis, Data Visualization, Dashboard Development, Healthcare Analytics, Business Insights
